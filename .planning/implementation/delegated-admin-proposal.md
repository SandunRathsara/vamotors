A readable account/role is not an editable or assignable one. Staff without those capabilities remains denied.

### Exact ceiling test (small, conservative implication check)

Use the actor's **pre-mutation current positive grants**, not type-level `can`, role names, browser presentationGrants or the proposed post-change ability. Same subject and action only: update does not imply create, read does not imply export, and grantable administrative capabilities themselves must pass the same test. Union can supply different fields from different roles.

1. Validate using the existing closed subject/action/field/condition registry. Reject omitted fields, unknown paths/operators/conditions and inverted rules with `422 INVALID_INPUT`. `fields:[]` confers nothing; no arbitrary strings. Expand `*` and parent paths against the current subject/action universe for comparison, respecting A1F subtree versus leaf semantics. Normalizing `*` to named paths does NOT freeze authority: a retained `financial` parent still expands to future children in abilityFor/expanded. Every registry-universe change therefore requires deliberate revalidation/migration of affected stored grants (including protected system grants) and review of ceilings/registry projection before new paths become configurable/effective. Do not claim frozen grants or silently alter A1F parent semantics.
2. For each proposed grant and each expanded field, find at least one actor grant for the **same subject/action/field** whose condition conjunction is implied by the proposed conjunction. Absence of conditions means all records; adding an equality narrows; removing/changing a required equality broadens and fails. For literal conjunctions, actor predicates must be a subset of identical proposed predicates. No arbitrary Boolean/SAT engine: if no single covering conjunction exists for that field, reject conservatively even if a complicated union might theoretically cover it. Different fields may have different witnesses; never merge their conditions into a broader rule.
3. Resolve the granting actor's `$actor.id` to the granting user's trusted ID. A stored role's `$actor.id` binds separately to **each eventual holder**. Therefore copying an “own records” condition is NOT a subset merely because its text matches. For reusable roles, treat recipient identity as arbitrary: an own-only actor cannot create/edit/assign such a transferable own-only grant unless an independent covering actor grant proves it for every holder (normally an unconditional grant, possibly another shared literal condition). Do not freeze the grant to the grantor's ID, rewrite ownership, or check only today's assignees. An actor with unconditional Customer.read on phone can delegate Customer.read/phone with createdBy=$actor.id; an actor with only own-customer phone access cannot delegate it to someone else. This also covers Notification.recipientUserId; no notification exception bypass.
4. Apply coverage only where the EXECUTION table confers/proposes authority, not as an old-grant floor for removals/reductions/archive/rename. Inactive role grant edits still validate proposed stored authority for eventual use; empty/inactive roles never evade systemKey protection. Excessive proposed authority fails `403 FORBIDDEN`; unauthorized changed fields fail `403 FIELD_FORBIDDEN`; hidden target/ref stays404. No partial grant stripping or partially applied save.

This favors a checkable subset over a CASL rewrite. Existing execution/read projection keeps the current ability/registry. Administrator can configure valid conditional roles without the delegate ceiling, but cannot bypass system constraints. Granted roles are independent RBAC grants, **not revocable delegation leases**: later loss of the grantor's business permission does not revoke another user's valid role. Future executions use the new ceiling where required; replays use REPLAY-REFERENCE only. No cascading provenance/revocation.

### Protected authority and self-change

- Protected `ADMINISTRATOR` systemKey is server-owned/unique/immutable; no role archive, rename, replacement of grants, deactivation or removal through generic save. Preserve current stricter system-role guard, not just “cannot delete.” Direct-runtime writes must retain existing DB protection.

A delegate may edit a permitted profile field only when activation/membership are unchanged and changed fields/required refs are authorized. No protected role clone gets protected identity; systemKey stays unassignable by payload.
- Do not invent a blanket self-edit ban. A self-assignment or edit to a role the caller holds is tested against the caller's **pre-change** authority; a net increase beyond that ceiling fails, including indirect role edits and reactivation. Permitted self-reduction/deactivation may succeed; the next request is denied and UI clears private caches. Other holders of an edited role do not enlarge the editor's ceiling.
- Administrator self-removal/deactivation remains allowed only when at least one **other distinct active** protected Administrator survives. Lock AdminGuard then relevant auth/account/role rows in established sorted order, recompute final graph and invariant, preserve immutable admin management grants and DB triggers; final active admin loss is `409 LAST_ADMIN`. Two concurrent removals cannot both pass. Keep the verified shared transaction owner's final `SET CONSTRAINTS ALL IMMEDIATE` rollback/release path. No role-assignment count substituted for active-user count.

## Current authority, replay and concurrency

Reuse currentActor and existing AdminGuard serialization, not a session-cached ceiling. Preserve `lock_admin_guard()`'s **UPDATE generation = generation + 1**, not merely SELECT FOR UPDATE: under SERIALIZABLE this forces stale waiters to abort/retry. After the guard, reload identity/activation/roles/rules and relevant target/role rows on every attempt. Assignment/reactivation/grant edits and revocation share this guard. Expected Role/User revisions protect stale input, not authorization. Existing A3R refs remain; removed roles resolve internally under lock, with no invented client expected refs.

Revocation committed before that lock wins; already-linearized command may finish. On retry, reconstruct authority and subset witnesses; never reuse a pretransaction result. Keep A2 lock order, bounded transient retries, revision increments/authz fingerprint, business+audit+receipt atomicity, and final-graph constraints. Missing/stale required revisions yield428/409 only after authorization, without leaking hidden refs.

**REPLAY-REFERENCE is separate from execution.** Authenticate fresh active actor under the guard; identify receipt by environment/actorKey/command/key, validate canonical keyed digest and trusted result.type/id binding, and perform exactly the table's current action/record + read-reference checks. Every returned result/affected ref requires current read id/revision; deny404 if hidden,403 if relevant action denied. Required response fields cannot be redacted into a misleading success: deny if unavailable. Input role refs are checked for existence/current read id/revision, NOT current grants or active/expected-revision equality. No old grants/delta are stored in the receipt, so never derive them from today's assignments or role contents. No mutation transfer is re-executed and **no reauthorization of the historical mutation delta is promised**. No business/assignment/grant mutation, revision bump, audit/receipt insertion or last-admin transition occurs. The existing guard generation UPDATE is synchronization only, not a business effect; retain it for fresh authority.

Use existing receipt `{inputHash, result:{type,id,revision}}` and validated matching envelope: createUser/saveRole-create get result identity from receipt; updateUser/saveRole-existing/archiveRole also require it equals input.id. Return original commandId and committed result reference (including committed revision) with replayed=true; affected contains the same authorized entity's CURRENT revision. No raw entity/grants/credential fields are returned by Applied. Any future nested payload needs separate current projection/reference authorization. A hidden required ref blocks disclosure, not just the nested field. An action/read lost after self-reduction can deny replay without undoing prior success; loss of unrelated historical business grants alone cannot. Newly protected/currently archived result does not trigger mutation-only guards on replay. Changed envelope returns409 IDEMPOTENCY_MISMATCH only after safe current action/result/ref authorization; no receipt output or current revision leaked on failed authorization.
