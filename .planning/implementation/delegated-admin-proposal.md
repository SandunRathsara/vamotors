## Recommendation: existing capabilities plus attenuated grants

No new delegation table, role hierarchy, deny-rule language, generic policy engine or additional business scope. Administrator remains bootstrap/full administrator subject to all system invariants. A non-Administrator needs the existing User/Role command action and every submitted command field on the actual target (candidate on create), independently of this grant-ceiling test. A readable account/role is not an editable or assignable one. An ordinary staff member without those capabilities remains denied.

### Command/record scope

| Operation | Proposed non-Administrator rule, in addition to ordinary capability/field checks |
| --- | --- |
| createUser | User.create on candidate and all input fields; each assigned Role active, readable for required refs and grants, non-system, and fully covered by the actor's current grant ceiling. No credential read capability results from password-create permission. |
| updateUser profile only | User.update on actual DTO and submitted fields; no extra ceiling check for unchanged assignments. Retained role refs must still be authorized for the full-replacement form; do not silently drop hidden assignments. No new email/password edit or arbitrary patch endpoint. |
| updateUser activation | Reactivation requires every effective assigned role to be covered: otherwise enabling a dormant powerful account launders authority. Deactivation requires existing User.update/active, not possession of the target's unrelated business grants; protected Administrator-account rule below still applies. |
| updateUser assignments | Check each role in the symmetric difference (addition **and** removal), including its complete current grants, against the ceiling; newly added role must be active. Unchanged roles are not implicitly reassigned and cannot confer new authority. Require Role.read of refs and grants for changed roles. Removal of a stronger role goes to Administrator rather than allowing delegated interference with stronger authority. Existing required input Role revisions remain required; resolve removed-role refs server-side under lock. |
| saveRole create | Role.create/name+grants; all proposed grants covered; no caller-controlled systemKey, actor, revision or ownership. |
| saveRole existing | Role.update/name+grants on actual role, Role.read of grants/required refs; **both old and proposed full grant sets** covered, even for name-only save. This prevents taking over a stronger role by replacing its rules. All assignees receive the edit immediately; no invented per-assignee approval workflow. |
| archiveRole | Role.archive/active and readable role refs/grants; old grants covered. No physical deletion; system role prohibition below. |

The initial registry has **no User or Role condition keys** (`conditionKeys.User/Role=[]`). Thus a configured User/Role action presently covers that entire subject's records, with the explicit system constraints here; it cannot be represented as “only my staff” or “only roles I created.” Preserve actual-record authorization, but do not claim narrower administrative row scopes that the accepted runtime cannot express. Adding such keys/metadata needs a separate contract/schema decision, not a UI-only filter. Domain records retain their already-supported scoped conditions.

### Exact ceiling test (small, conservative implication check)

Use the actor's **pre-mutation current positive grants**, not type-level `can`, role names, browser presentationGrants or the proposed post-change ability. Same subject and action only: update does not imply create, read does not imply export, and grantable administrative capabilities themselves must pass the same test. Union can supply different fields from different roles.

1. Validate using the existing closed subject/action/field/condition registry. Reject omitted fields, unknown paths/operators/conditions and inverted rules with `422 INVALID_INPUT`. `fields:[]` confers nothing; no permission to smuggle arbitrary strings. Expand `*` and parent paths against the current subject/action universe, respecting subtree versus leaf semantics in A1F. Normalize accepted delegated grants to explicit paths so a future registry addition cannot silently widen an old `*` grant; version any registry-universe change deliberately.
2. For each proposed grant and each expanded field, find at least one actor grant for the **same subject/action/field** whose condition conjunction is implied by the proposed conjunction. Absence of conditions means all records; adding an equality narrows; removing/changing a required equality broadens and fails. For literal conjunctions, actor predicates must be a subset of identical proposed predicates. No arbitrary Boolean/SAT engine: if no single covering conjunction exists for that field, reject conservatively even if a complicated union might theoretically cover it. Different fields may have different witnesses; never merge their conditions into a broader rule.
3. Resolve the granting actor's `$actor.id` to the granting user's trusted ID. A stored role's `$actor.id` binds separately to **each eventual holder**. Therefore copying an “own records” condition is NOT a subset merely because its text matches. For reusable roles, treat recipient identity as arbitrary: an own-only actor cannot create/edit/assign such a transferable own-only grant unless an independent covering actor grant proves it for every holder (normally an unconditional grant, possibly another shared literal condition). Do not freeze the grant to the grantor's ID, rewrite ownership, or check only today's assignees. An actor with unconditional Customer.read on phone can delegate Customer.read/phone with createdBy=$actor.id; an actor with only own-customer phone access cannot delegate it to someone else. This also covers Notification.recipientUserId; no notification exception bypass.
4. Apply the same conservative test to old roles when required by the table. Empty/inactive roles do not evade systemKey protection; an inactive role's stored rules still matter for role edit/removal validation. Proposed excessive grant fails `403 FORBIDDEN`; unauthorized submitted fields fail `403 FIELD_FORBIDDEN`; hidden target/ref stays404. No partial grant stripping or partially applied role save.

This deliberately favors a checkable subset over a general CASL rewrite. Existing command execution and read projection continue using the current ability/registry. Administrator can configure valid conditional roles without the non-Administrator ceiling, but cannot bypass system constraints. Previously granted roles are normal independent RBAC grants, **not revocable delegation leases**: later loss of the grantor's business permission does not automatically revoke another user's validly granted role. Future commands/replays by that grantor use the new ceiling. Cascading grant provenance/revocation is not specified and is not proposed.

### Protected authority and self-change

- Protected `ADMINISTRATOR` systemKey is server-owned/unique/immutable; no role archive, rename, replacement of grants, deactivation or removal through generic save. Preserve current stricter system-role guard, not just “cannot delete.” Direct-runtime writes must retain existing DB protection.
- Only a current protected Administrator may add/remove Administrator membership or activate/deactivate an account holding it, even if the account is currently inactive. A non-Administrator may edit a permitted profile field on such an account only when activation/membership are unchanged and all submitted fields/required refs are authorized. No protected role “clone” gets the protected identity. Ceiling checks prevent a delegate constructing a more powerful custom role; systemKey stays unassignable by payload.
- Do not invent a blanket self-edit ban. A self-assignment or edit to a role the caller holds is tested against the caller's **pre-change** authority; a net increase beyond that ceiling fails, including indirect role edits and reactivation. Permitted self-reduction/deactivation may succeed; the next request is denied and UI clears private caches. Other holders of an edited role do not enlarge the editor's ceiling.
- Administrator self-removal/deactivation remains allowed only when at least one **other distinct active** protected Administrator survives. Lock AdminGuard then relevant auth/account/role rows in established sorted order, recompute final graph and invariant, preserve immutable admin management grants and DB triggers; final active admin loss is `409 LAST_ADMIN`. Two concurrent removals cannot both pass. Keep the verified shared transaction owner's final `SET CONSTRAINTS ALL IMMEDIATE` rollback/release path. No role-assignment count substituted for active-user count.

## Current authority, replay and concurrency

Reuse currentActor and the existing AdminGuard serialization, not a session-cached grant ceiling. Reload identity/activation/roles/rules under the command's authorization lock and reread changed/retained/removed roles, target activation and membership as relevant. Role edit versus assignment/reactivation must share this guard; expected Role/User revisions still protect stale input, not authorization. Existing A3R references remain; any new client expected-ref requirement for removed roles would need an explicit typed-contract change, not a hidden T03b requirement. Resolve their current rules internally now.

Revocation committed before that lock wins; already-linearized command may finish. On retry, reconstruct authority and subset witnesses; never reuse a pretransaction result. Keep A2 lock order, bounded transient retries, revision increments/authz fingerprint, business+audit+receipt atomicity, and final-graph constraints. Missing/stale required revisions yield428/409 only after authorization, without leaking hidden refs.

Same-key/same-envelope replay creates no effects and does not rerun “would this edit execute today” state/revision/last-admin transitions. It must reauthorize current activation, command capabilities, actual target/required fields and safe receipt references, plus current delegation ceiling for requested role/grant changes. If the role/target is now hidden, protected, or exceeds the ceiling, deny instead of returning the saved payload. Self-reduction may therefore make replay forbidden; that does not undo its prior commit. Changed-envelope key is409 mismatch after safe authorization. Never reveal old output using historical grants; retain original command/result identity with current authorized revision/projection only. Cover create-result actual rows as well as candidates, not candidate-only checks.

## Administrative reads and filtered registry

- `currentActor` stays self-only/minimized; no permission to list accounts. After policy acceptance only, replace coarse query Administrator gating with User.read/Role.read action + actual row/field enforcement; write capabilities are not necessary to read a permitted administrative list. Mutation access remains independently checked. Current User/Role no-condition universe means all rows or none per subject; do not pretend client filtering adds security.
- Use readScope before count, search/filter/sort and pagination, then project DTO fields. Unknown query keys422; forbidden search/sort/filter fields403, including default ordering by id if id unreadable (or choose an explicitly authorized contract-supported order, never silently leak). No fetch-all/page-filter. Hidden detail404. Roles.grants requires its own read permission; nested role links/names/refs also require target Role.read and respective fields, in addition to User.roles. Omit unavailable links with redaction metadata. No raw include graph, credential fields or account/session payload in hydration/JSON/audit/export.
- Last-admin metadata requires authorized User administration and User.read/adminProtection; count privileged users internally only to calculate the authorized flag. Do not return global role-assignee counts without read permission for the whole population. Audit queries need AuditLog.read row scope **before count/page**, original entity read, and per-diff field checks (including nested role/grant values). Current audit handler's final projection alone is not a complete list gate. Preserve no-store on success/error across HTTP/RSC.
- Registry entry gate remains A1Q: Role.read plus Role.update, or a User administration capability (create/update). Filter each subject/action/path to the same ceiling/configurability test used by commands. Role editing requires the corresponding Role command fields; an account-only delegate can discover only the covered assignable vocabulary, not gain Role.create/update. Omit subjects/actions with no paths; do not expose `*` unless every expanded path is covered. Show only allowed equality keys/value choices; condition-only coverage must not be presented as an unconditional field grant. All command payloads are revalidated server-side even if the registry UI hid the choice.
- Current PermissionRegistryDTO expresses grammar, not coupled per-field grant ceilings or per-actor literal predicates. Smallest no-DTO-change option: return a conservative grammar subset, restrict finite allowedValues where representable, and omit conditionally-only fields/unsafe actor-relative choices that cannot be accurately represented. Explain those omissions in the editor; existing roles are still viewable with Role.read/grants and may be valid under command checks. If complete guided editing of every conditional-only grant is required, add an independently reviewed DTO for field+condition templates; do not leak all grammar and call it filtered. No such API change authorized here.

## Implementation seams and decision

At base, `lib/server/admin.ts` has coarse requireAdministrator twice; validateRoles checks only Role.read id/revision, not grant attenuation. updateUser replaces all memberships; saveRole replaces all grants; replays skip state/revision checks and reauthorize output refs. `lib/server/queries.ts` gates registry/users/roles/audit by Administrator, correctly scopes User/Role lists but registry returns its full grammar and audit counts precede full authorization. `lib/server/permissions.ts` already owns grantSchema/fieldsFor/abilityFor/project/readScope/permissionRegistryDTO; User/Role conditions are empty. `lib/server/actor.ts` already loads current activation/roles/grants and authz fingerprint. Reuse these seams with a single bounded subset helper and command policy checks; do not replace CASL, add a second authority or change schema.

Options:

1. **Retain Administrator-only administration**: safest immediate implementation, USER wording directly supports it, but does not complete T03b's intended delegated surface/A1Q filtering intent. This remains the live guard until agreement.
2. **Recommended after explicit acceptance: capability + conservative ceiling above**, existing grammar and conservative registry projection; no new business restrictions, schema or general implication engine. Administrator handles nonrepresentable/higher authority cases.
3. Explicit independent grant-authority ACL/provenance or new conditional-template DTO: more expressive but larger than the evidenced need. Defer unless the user requires delegation beyond the actor's own authority, cascaded revocation, narrower administrative populations, or fully guided conditional-only editing.

**Real unresolved user-policy decision:** are non-Administrator users allowed to administer accounts/roles when explicitly granted the existing User/Role capabilities, bounded as option2, or is administration reserved to protected Administrator membership (option1)? USER-01/03 and the matrix alone do not decide this; A1Q/T03b signal intent but do not define the anti-escalation agreement. Parent must obtain/record that explicit agreement after separate independent review. This proposal recommends option2, not permission to ship it. All implementation scenarios above remain unexecuted; no G1/feature acceptance follows from documentation checks.
